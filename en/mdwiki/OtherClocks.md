### Incandescent Binary Clock

Crazy idea for a binary clock using small incandescent bulbs.
<br>[GitHub](https://github.com/eshazen/transistor-clock)

Bulb count (20):

* (2) 10 Hours: 0-2 
* (4) 1 Hours 0-9
* (3) 10 minutes 0-5
* (4) 1 minutes 0-9
* (3) 10 seconds 0-5
* (4) 1 secondss 0-9
